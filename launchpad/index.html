LAUNCHPAD BRIEF · PRODUCT DESIGN & UXR

I built the prototype. Now I need to cut it down to a product.

I am exploring a more flexible way to receive parcels in Japan. I already built a working prototype — and I am fairly sure it is three products wide and zero products deep. I want help choosing what to keep, and help figuring out how to validate it honestly. 日本での、もっと柔軟な荷物の受け取り方を検討しています。すでに動くプロトタイプを作りましたが、たぶん「横に3製品ぶん広くて、縦に1製品ぶんもない」状態です。何を残すかの判断と、正直な検証のやり方を相談したいです。

Eigo “Ayden” Kono Hitachi, Ltd. · Tokyo OpenAI DevDay · Sep 29, 2026

What I told you when I applied

WHAT ARE YOU BUILDING?

I’m exploring a new delivery experience in Japan to reduce failed deliveries and redeliveries. While doorstep delivery is common in the U.S., Japan still relies more on direct handoffs, creating redeliveries when recipients are unavailable. I want to explore a service that lets people receive packages more flexibly based on when and where they are available.

WHAT WOULD YOU LIKE TO DISCUSS?

How should I validate this idea and design the simplest first product? I’d like to explore how AI could match recipients’ availability and preferred locations with delivery operations, while creating a simple experience that both customers and delivery providers would actually use.

Everything below is the detail behind those two paragraphs. 以下はこの2段落の中身です。応募時に書いた内容と食い違わないようにしています。

What I have already built

A working prototype you can open on your phone right now. It simulates a parcel in transit and lets you move where it lands — a station locker, a convenience store, your office, a friend, or to you while you are still walking home. A policy engine decides whether that move is allowed and returns the reason when it is not. いますぐスマホで開ける動くプロトタイプです。配送中の荷物の受取先を差し替えられます。駅のロッカー、コンビニ、勤務先、友人、そして帰宅途中の自分自身。移動先が許されるかどうかをポリシーエンジンが判定し、だめなときは理由を返します。

  • ✗ Zero conversations with a delivery company. I do not know what one redelivery costs them, and that number decides whether any of this is a business. 配送事業者との会話がゼロです。再配達1件のコストを知らないのに、その数字が事業になるかを決めます。
  • ✗ No real users yet. The survey exists; nobody has filled it in. 実ユーザーがまだいません。アンケートはありますが、誰も回答していません。

What I want your help with

Q1 · VALIDATION

Which side do I validate first — and what test could actually kill this? どちら側を先に検証すべきで、どんなテストならこのアイデアを否定できるのか？

This needs recipients and delivery companies. If I only talk to recipients I will hear “that sounds convenient” forever and learn nothing. What is the cheapest test that would genuinely change my mind? And is a survey attached to a prototype real evidence, or am I just measuring politeness? これは受け取る人と配送事業者の両方が必要です。受け取る人にだけ聞けば「便利そう」と永遠に言われ、何も学べません。本当に考えを変えさせるいちばん安いテストは何でしょうか。そしてプロトタイプに付けたアンケートは証拠になるのか、それとも社交辞令を測っているだけなのでしょうか。

Why it matters: I have 7 days of event and then my evenings. I can run exactly one validation loop before I lose momentum. 使える時間はイベントと、あとは夜と週末だけです。勢いを失う前に回せる検証は1周ぶんしかありません。
Q2 · SIMPLEST FIRST PRODUCT

What would you cut? Which single thing ships first? 何を削りますか？ 最初に出す1つは何でしょうか？

I can show you the prototype and you can point at what to delete. My instinct says pick one item type and one place — for example “frozen groceries, to a station locker on your commute” — and throw the other seven away. But I do not trust my own instinct here, because every feature I built felt necessary while I was building it. プロトタイプをお見せするので、消すところを指してもらえます。私の直感は「1つの品目 × 1つの場所」、たとえば「冷凍食品を、通勤経路の駅ロッカーへ」に絞って残り7つを捨てることです。ただ自分の直感を信用していません。作っている最中は、どの機能も必要に見えたからです。

Why it matters: A platform needs partners on day one. A single use case might only need one locker operator and 20 people. 基盤は初日からパートナーが必要ですが、ユースケースを1つに絞れば、ロッカー事業者1社と20人で始められるかもしれません。
Q3 · TWO-SIDED EXPERIENCE

How do you design one experience for two sides that want opposite things? 正反対のものを求める2者に、1つの体験をどう設計するのか？

The recipient wants to change their mind late — ideally while the van is already moving. Operations wants route density, predictable stops and no surprises. Where does the flexibility have to stop so that a driver’s day still works? Is there a version where the driver experiences less chaos, not more? 受け取る人は、できれば車が走り出したあとでも予定を変えたい。配送側はルートの密度と、予測できる停車と、驚きがないことを求めます。ドライバーの1日が成立するために、柔軟さはどこで止まるべきでしょうか。ドライバーにとって混乱が「減る」形はありうるでしょうか。

Why it matters: If the carrier experiences this as more variability, they will never adopt it, and the recipient side becomes worthless. 配送事業者にとって「変動が増えるもの」に見えたら採用されず、受け取る人側の価値もゼロになります。
Q4 · WHERE AI ACTUALLY BELONGS

Does the first version need AI at all? 最初のバージョンに、そもそもAIは必要なのか？

I wrote in my application that AI could match availability and preferred locations with delivery operations, and I still think that is where this goes. But I want to ask the uncomfortable version of the question at an OpenAI event: is v1 just a good scheduling interface, with AI arriving only once there is enough behaviour to predict? If AI does belong in v1 — is it the matching, the prediction of where I will be, or the conversation that replaces the scheduling UI entirely? 応募文には「AIが、受取可能な時間と場所を配送オペレーションと突き合わせられる」と書きましたし、最終的にはそこに行くと今も思っています。ただ、OpenAIのイベントで居心地の悪い形の問いを聞きたいです。v1は単に良いスケジュール調整のUIで、AIは予測できるだけの行動データが溜まってから来るのではないか。もしv1にAIが入るなら、それはマッチングなのか、居場所の予測なのか、それともスケジュール画面そのものを置き換える対話なのか。

Why it matters: I would rather be told “not yet” by you than find out after building it. 作ったあとで気づくより、いま「まだ早い」と言われた方がいいです。

My own current guess (tell me if it is wrong)

    KEEP One item type where failure actually hurts: chilled and frozen groceries. You cannot leave them at a door, so the problem is real and unsolved. 失敗が本当に痛む品目を1つ：冷蔵・冷凍食品。置き配にできないので、課題が実在していて未解決です。
  • KEEP One place that already exists: a temperature-controlled locker on a commute. No new hardware, no new building access. すでに存在する場所を1つ：通勤経路上の温度帯対応ロッカー。新しい機器も建物への立ち入り権限も要りません。
  • CUT Delivery to a moving person, delegation to friends, robot handover, indoor delivery, the autonomy levels, the whole permission model. All of it is interesting. None of it is v1. 移動中の人への配送、友人への委任、ロボットとの受渡し、屋内搬入、自律レベル、権限モデル全体。どれも面白いですが、v1ではありません。
  • TEST Whether anyone will walk 3 extra minutes for a parcel they could have had at home tomorrow. That single behaviour decides everything. 明日自宅で受け取れる荷物のために、3分多く歩く人がいるか。この1つの行動がすべてを決めます。

Context you may not have about Japan

    Handoff is cultural, not just regulatory Direct handoff has been the norm for security reasons. Unattended delivery is now standard but many people still dislike it. 対面での受け渡しは防犯上の理由で長く標準でした。置き配は制度化されましたが、抵抗のある人はまだ多いです。
  • Carriers are already at the limit Driver shortage plus a projected 34% capacity shortfall by 2030 means reducing redelivery is now a continuity problem, not a cost-saving nice-to-have. ドライバー不足に加え、2030年に輸送能力が34%不足する見通しです。再配達削減はコスト削減ではなく事業継続の問題になっています。
  • Each carrier has its own app Changing a pickup location is already possible — per carrier, in silos. A cross-carrier layer is the opportunity and also the political problem. 受取場所の変更は各社のアプリで既に可能ですが、会社ごとに分断されています。横断できることが機会であり、同時に政治的な難所です。
  • Lockers already exist at scale A carrier-neutral open locker network is already at roughly 6,000 locations. I do not need to build boxes. キャリア横断のオープンロッカーが既に約6,000カ所あります。箱を作る必要はありません。

What I will do with your answer

    1 Cut the prototype down to whatever we agree ships first — within the week. 合意した「最初に出すもの」までプロトタイプを削ります。その週のうちに。
  • 2 Run the validation test you suggest, and send you the result — including if it fails. 提案された検証を実行し、結果を送ります。失敗した場合もそのまま送ります。
  • 3 Get the cost of one redelivery from an actual carrier. That is my single biggest unknown. 再配達1件のコストを、実際の配送事業者から取ってきます。これが最大の未知です。

If you are another builder in this group: I would like to talk to anyone working on two-sided products where one side is an operations team. The design problem looks identical from where I sit. 同じグループの他の参加者へ：片側が現場オペレーションである二面性のプロダクトを扱っている方と話したいです。私の立場からは、設計上の課題がまったく同じに見えています。

Spoken script (for me)

Read once before the session. Slashes are breath marks. セッション前に一度音読する用。スラッシュは息継ぎの位置。

20 seconds — who I am, what I am building
“I’m Eigo Kono / people call me Ayden. I work at Hitachi / in Tokyo. In Japan / parcels are still handed over in person / so when you’re not home / it comes back tomorrow. Four hundred million parcels a year get redelivered. I’m building a way to receive a parcel / based on when and where you can actually be / instead of one fixed address.”
15 seconds — the ask
“I already built a prototype / and I think it’s too big. So my questions are / what would you cut to get to a first product / how do I validate this when I need both recipients and delivery companies / and honestly / does version one even need AI?”
10 seconds — show it
“Can I show you? / It takes twenty seconds.” → open the prototype, tap the station locker, then tap the friend option on the prescription medicine so they see the policy engine refuse it.
If they ask “why you?”
“I’m not a logistics person / I’m the customer who kept missing deliveries / and I can build. I work at a company that builds infrastructure in Japan / so I can get into the room with carriers.”
If the answer is “this is not a business”
“That’s useful / tell me why. Is it the willingness to pay / or the two-sided cold start?” → do not defend. Write down the reason.
